Evacuation & First-Aid Checklist

Remain calm and assess your surroundings immediately
Follow the designated evacuation route shown on your device
Move upwind and uphill away from flood zones or fire
Do not use elevators — use stairs only
Help elderly or disabled people in your vicinity
Stay connected — keep your phone charged and on
Proceed to the nearest safe zone as directed
If injured, stop bleeding by applying firm pressure with a cloth
Do not move someone with a suspected spinal injury
For unconscious person — check breathing and perform CPR if needed
Treat burns with cool (not cold) running water for 10 minutes
Keep the injured person warm and calm until help arrives
Do not give food or water to an unconscious person
Grab your emergency kit — water, food, documents, medication
Take a fully charged phone and portable power bank
Wear sturdy shoes and protective clothing
Carry a flashlight or use your phone torch
Keep cash in small denominations — ATMs may be unavailable
Bring copies of important documents in a waterproof bag
